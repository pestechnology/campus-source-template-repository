# 🤝 Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## 🌟 Our Pledge
We, as contributors and maintainers, pledge to make participation in our project and our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

---

## ✅ Behavior Standards
We encourage the following behaviors that contribute to a positive environment:
- **Demonstrating empathy** and kindness toward other people.
- **Being respectful** of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences.
- **Giving and gracefully accepting** constructive feedback.
- **Accepting responsibility** and apologizing to those affected by our mistakes, and learning from the experience.
- **Focusing on what is best** not just for us as individuals, but for the overall community.

### 🚫 Unacceptable Behavior
- The use of sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention or advances.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address, without their explicit permission.
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

---

## 📩 Reporting a Violation
Community members are encouraged to report any behavior that violates these standards.

| Method | Link / Contact | Purpose |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Email Support** | [conduct@[CUSTOMIZE].dev](mailto:conduct@[CUSTOMIZE].dev) | Direct reporting to maintainers. |
| **Discord DM** | `@maintainer_handle` | Fast, private escalation. |
| **Anonymous Form** | [Report anonymously here](https://[CUSTOMIZE].dev/conduct/report) | For high-sensitivity reports. |

All reports will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. All maintainers are obligated to maintain confidentiality with regard to the reporter of an incident.

---

## ⚖️ Enforcement Table

Project maintainers follow these guidelines in determining the consequences for any action they deem in violation of this Code of Conduct:

| Level | Action | Description |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Level 1** | **Correction** | A private, written warning, providing clarity around the nature of the violation and an explanation of why the behavior was inappropriate. |
| **Level 2** | **Warning** | A warning with consequences for continued behavior. No interaction with the people involved, including unsolicited interaction with those enforcing the Code of Conduct, for a specified period of time. This includes avoiding interactions in social media as well as community channels like Discord. |
| **Level 3** | **Suspension** | A temporary ban from any sort of interaction or communication with the community for a specified period of time. No public or private interaction with the people involved, including unsolicited interaction with those enforcing the Code of Conduct, is allowed. |
| **Level 4** | **Expulsion** | A permanent ban from any sort of public interaction within the community. |
