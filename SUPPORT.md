# Support and Communication

We are committed to providing a structured support ecosystem for our contributors. Please utilize the following channels based on the nature of your inquiry.

---

## Technical Support

-   **Discord**: For real-time troubleshooting and development synchronization. [Join the Server](https://discord.gg/[CUSTOMIZE])
-   **Channel Guide**:
    -   `#help-and-support`: Local environment issues and code-level hurdles.
    -   `#bug-reports`: Verification and reporting of unexpected behavior in the codebase.

## Project Governance & Proposals

-   **GitHub Discussions**: Use this for high-level technical proposals (RFCs), architectural questions, and community announcements.
-   **Issues**: Track specific, actionable tasks. Ensure you utilize the [Issue Templates](.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/) for all submissions.

## Official Inquiries

-   **Email**: For administrative matters, partnerships, or reporting sensitive issues.
    -   [contribute@[CUSTOMIZE].dev](mailto:contribute@[CUSTOMIZE].dev)
-   **Newsletter**: Stay informed on major project milestones and contributor spotlights. [Subscribe here](https://[CUSTOMIZE].dev/newsletter)

---

## Response Times
Maintainers aim to respond to all technical inquiries within **48–72 hours**. For critical issues or administrative requests, please expect a response within **2 business days**.
