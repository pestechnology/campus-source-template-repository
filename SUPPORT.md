# 🆘 Support & Community

Need help? Have a question? We're here for you!

## 💬 Discord (Virtual Office)
Join our Discord server for real-time collaboration. We use the following channels:
- `#introductions`: Say hi and share your student profile.
- `#project-help`: Technical debugging and mentorship.
- `#pull-requests`: Request reviews or celebrate your merge.
- `#announcements`: Important project and campus updates.

👉 [Join our Discord](https://discord.gg/campus-events)

## 📩 Email Support
For official inquiries or private matters, reach us at:
- **Project Support**: [contact@campusevents.dev](mailto:contact@campusevents.dev)
- **Direct Lead**: [leader@yourcampus.dev](mailto:leader@yourcampus.dev)

## 📁 GitHub Channels
- **Discussions**: Use [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/campus-source/template/discussions) for long-form questions or RFCs.
- **Issues**: Use [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/campus-source/template/issues) to report bugs or request specific features.

## ❓ FAQ
**Q: I'm a beginner, can I still contribute?**
A: Absolutely! We welcome students of all skill levels. Check out our `good first issue` tags.

**Q: Can I use this template for my university project?**
A: Yes! That's exactly what it's for.
