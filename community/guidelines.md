# 🤝 Community Guidelines

Our community is built on trust, respect, and mutual growth. Beyond our [Code of Conduct](../CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md), these guidelines help us work better together.

## 💬 Discussion Etiquette
- **Ask once**: Post your question in the appropriate Discord channel.
- **Be descriptive**: Provide logs, screenshots, and context when asking for help.
- **Help others**: If you see someone struggling with something you've solved, jump in and help!

## 🗓️ Weekly Sync Meetings
We host community-wide syncs to align on the roadmap.
- **When**: Every Saturday, 7:00 PM IST
- **Where**: Discord Voice Channel (`#community-call`)
- **Agenda**: Demos, Blockers, and Planning.

## 🚀 Decision Making
Major project decisions (architectural changes, new tech stack) are discussed in **GitHub Discussions**. Every contributor has a voice, but the final decision rests with the Maintainers.
