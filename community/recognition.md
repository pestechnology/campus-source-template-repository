# 🏅 Contribution Recognition

At Campus Source, we believe that every contribution—no matter how small—should be celebrated. Here is how we recognize our community members:

## 📜 All-Contributors Wall
We maintain a list of everyone who has helped the project in [CONTRIBUTORS.md](../CONTRIBUTORS.md). This includes:
- Code commits
- Documentation fixes
- Bug reports
- Help in Discord

## 🏷️ Achievement Badges
We use GitHub labels and profile badges to highlight specific roles:
- **Core Contributor**: Students who have merged 5+ major PRs.
- **Top Tester**: Students who consistently find and report critical bugs.
- **Documentation Hero**: Students who improve our guides and onboarding.

## 🎓 Verified Campus Credits
For many student contributors, we can provide letters of appreciation or digital certificates that can be added to LinkedIn or shared with university departments.
