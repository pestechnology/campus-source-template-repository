# 🤝 Community Meetings

We hold regular meetings to sync on project progress and welcome new members.

## 🗓️ Weekly Sync
- **Time**: Every Saturday, 7:00 PM IST
- **Location**: Discord Voice Channel (`#community-call`)

## 📋 Agenda
1. **Demos**: Show off what you've built this week.
2. **Blockers**: Get help with any technical hurdles.
3. **Planning**: Review the roadmap and assign new issues.
4. **Q&A**: Open floor for new contributors to ask questions.

---
*Can't make it? We post meeting notes in the `#announcements` channel on Discord!*
