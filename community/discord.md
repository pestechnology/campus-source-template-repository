# 💬 Discord Community

Join our Discord server to connect with other student contributors and maintainers!

## 🔗 Invite Link
[Join our Discord](https://discord.gg/yourlink)

## 🏗️ Server Channels
- `#announcements`: Important project and campus updates.
- `#general`: General community chat.
- `#project-discussion`: Deep dives into specific features.
- `#help-support`: Technical debugging and peer mentorship.
- `#beginner-issues`: Dedicated help for first-time contributors.
- `#contributors`: A place for the core team to sync.

## 🏅 Roles
- **Maintainer**: Project leads and decision-makers.
- **Core Contributor**: Regular contributors with deep project knowledge.
- **Student Contributor**: Everyone who has merged a PR!
